    Our approach

    Five phases. Zero guesswork.

    A structured work-breakdown from initiation to handover keeps scope, cost and quality under control at every stage.

    Full work breakdown
    01

    Phase 1 of 5

    Initiation

    Scope, team and site aligned before a single drawing is issued.

    • Appoint PM & MEP team
    • Define project scope & goals
    • Site survey & feasibility
    • Client & stakeholder buy-in
    02

    Phase 2 of 5

    Design & Planning

    Coordinated MEP design, programme and cost locked in before work starts.

    • MEP shop drawings & design
    • Schedule & manpower plan
    • Budget & cost planning
    • Material submittals & approval
    03

    Phase 3 of 5

    Procurement & Execution

    Supply and installation tracked against plan and coordinated with every trade.

    • Material procurement & logistics
    • HVAC, electrical & plumbing install
    • Progress tracking: plan vs actual
    • Coordination with other trades
    04

    Phase 4 of 5

    Testing & Control

    Every system tested, balanced and inspected before it is signed off.

    • Testing, commissioning & balancing
    • Quality inspections & snagging
    • Change & scope control
    • Documentation control
    05

    Phase 5 of 5

    Closure & Handover

    A clean handover with full documentation and warranty cover.

    • Final commissioning & handover
    • As-built drawings & O&M manuals
    • Defects liability & warranty
    • Close-out report & lessons learned
