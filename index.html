About BTGC

Meet Our Company

A UAE interior fit-out and contracting company that turns clients’ visions into inspiring, functional spaces — on time and to spec.

Since 2018 we have delivered design & build, fit-out, bespoke joinery, MEP, renovation and maintenance for commercial, retail, hospitality, healthcare and government clients — including the Ministry of Presidential Affairs, ICT, Aldar, Brand Folio and Dalma Mall.

  • Turnkey design & build

    One contract from concept drawings to handover.

  • In-house MEP engineering

    HVAC, electrical, PHE & ELV designed and installed by our teams.

  • ISO-certified quality & HSE

    ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001 management systems.

  • Abu Dhabi & Dubai teams

    60+ staff, our own warehouse and fast mobilisation.

Our services

What we do

Seven in-house disciplines, coordinated by one project team — so fit-out, MEP and finishing works move as one.

01

Interior & Fit-Out

Design-and-build interiors for offices, retail, cafés, gyms and villas — from concept to keys.

  • Interior design
  • Joinery & furniture
  • Lighting
  • Sanitary ware selection
02

Mechanical / HVAC

Heat-load calculation, HVAC design, ventilation and controls that keep buildings efficient.

  • Heat load calculations
  • HVAC system design & scheme development
  • Equipment selection & sizing
  • Air distribution & piping design
03

Electrical Works

System design, HT/LT distribution, backup power and panel engineering for safe operations.

  • Electrical scheme & system design
  • HT/LT distribution systems
  • Electrical load calculation
  • Back-up power arrangement
04

Plumbing & Fire (PHE)

Water supply, drainage, sewage treatment and fire hydrant & sprinkler systems.

  • Water demand calculations
  • Water storage & distribution
  • Drainage & waste systems
  • Sewage treatment plant
05

Civil Works

Screeding, masonry, tiling, gypsum, glazing and steel — finished with precision.

  • Floor screeding
  • Masonry & tile works
  • Gypsum & paint works
  • Aluminium & glass works
06

ELV / Low Current

Fire alarm, CCTV, access control, PA and integrated building management systems.

  • Fire alarm system
  • Public address system
  • Access control system
  • Integrated building management system
07

Maintenance

Planned and reactive maintenance for HVAC, electrical, plumbing and building fabric.

  • All kinds of building maintenance
  • All types of cleaning services
  • HVAC maintenance
  • Electrical maintenance
