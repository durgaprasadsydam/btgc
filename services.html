What we do

Every trade.
One team.

Seven in-house disciplines — designed, installed, tested and maintained by one accountable contractor.

01

Interior & Fit-Out

Our Interior & Fit-Out team turns empty shells into finished, branded spaces. We handle interior design, bespoke joinery and furniture, lighting and sanitary ware selection, and coordinate every trade on site so the space opens on schedule.

  • Interior design
  • Joinery & furniture
  • Lighting
  • Sanitary ware selection
  • Turnkey design & build
  • Renovation & refurbishment
02

Mechanical / HVAC

We provide complete mechanical engineering solutions, including heat load calculations, HVAC system design, equipment selection, air distribution, ventilation and pressurization systems, and HVAC controls — delivering efficient, reliable and sustainable building services.

  • Heat load calculations
  • HVAC system design & scheme development
  • Equipment selection & sizing
  • Air distribution & piping design
  • Ventilation systems
  • Staircase / lift-well pressurization
  • HVAC controls & automation
03

Electrical Works

We deliver comprehensive electrical engineering solutions, including system design, HT/LT distribution, electrical load calculations, backup power arrangements, cabling and bus riser systems, and panel scheduling with cable sizing for safe and efficient building operations.

  • Electrical scheme & system design
  • HT/LT distribution systems
  • Electrical load calculation
  • Back-up power arrangement
  • Cabling & bus riser systems
  • Panel schedules & cable sizing
04

Plumbing & Fire (PHE)

We deliver comprehensive plumbing and fire protection solutions, including water demand calculations, water storage and distribution, drainage and waste systems, sewage treatment plants, and fire hydrant and sprinkler systems for reliable and efficient building operations.

  • Water demand calculations
  • Water storage & distribution
  • Drainage & waste systems
  • Sewage treatment plant
  • Fire hydrant & sprinkler systems
05

Civil Works

Our Civil Works division provides reliable construction and finishing solutions for commercial, residential and industrial projects — delivered with a commitment to quality, safety and timely execution.

  • Floor screeding
  • Masonry & tile works
  • Gypsum & paint works
  • Aluminium & glass works
  • Steel structure works
  • Rubber flooring
06

ELV / Low Current

We design and install the low-current systems that make a building safe, secure and smart — integrated so facility teams can monitor and control everything from one place.

  • Fire alarm system
  • Public address system
  • Access control system
  • Integrated building management system
  • Telecommunication system
  • CCTV system
  • Parking management system
07

Maintenance

After handover we stay on. Our maintenance teams keep buildings running with planned and reactive services across every discipline — supported by our own warehouse and staff in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

  • All kinds of building maintenance
  • All types of cleaning services
  • HVAC maintenance
  • Electrical maintenance
  • Plumbing maintenance

Work breakdown structure

From survey to handover.

Our project managers and MEP team run each job through five controlled phases, with documented deliverables at every gate.

01

Initiation

  • →Appoint PM & MEP team
  • →Define project scope & goals
  • →Site survey & feasibility
  • →Client & stakeholder buy-in
02

Design & Planning

  • →MEP shop drawings & design
  • →Schedule & manpower plan
  • →Budget & cost planning
  • →Material submittals & approval
03

Procurement & Execution

  • →Material procurement & logistics
  • →HVAC, electrical & plumbing install
  • →Progress tracking: plan vs actual
  • →Coordination with other trades

  • →Quality inspections & snagging
  • →Change & scope control
  • →Documentation control

Closure & Handover

  • →Final commissioning & handover
  • →As-built drawings & O&M manuals
  • →Defects liability & warranty
