Our story

Turning visions into spaces that create lasting value.

Established in 2018, BTGC is a leading interior fit-out and contracting company in the UAE — recognised for exceptional interiors delivered through innovative design, superior craftsmanship and turnkey execution.

We deliver design & build, fit-out contracting, bespoke joinery, MEP, renovation and maintenance for commercial, corporate, retail, hospitality, healthcare and government clients — executed with precision and the highest standards of quality, safety and timely delivery.

Our success is built on integrity, teamwork and accountability — trusted by organisations including Aldar, KPMG, Al Rostamani Group, Anantara, Khidmah and Dalma Mall.
