    About us

    Our story

    Established 2018. Trusted by the UAE’s leading names.

    BTGC is a leading interior fit-out and contracting company in the United Arab Emirates, recognised for exceptional interior environments delivered through innovative design, superior craftsmanship and turnkey project execution.

    We provide design & build, fit-out contracting, bespoke joinery, renovation and general maintenance across commercial, corporate, retail, hospitality, healthcare and government sectors — every project executed with precision, safety and timely delivery.

    Over the years we have earned the trust of renowned organisations including the Ministry of Presidential Affairs, ICT, Aldar, Brand Folio and Dalma Mall. As we grow, we remain dedicated to setting new benchmarks in the UAE’s fit-out industry with tailored, value-driven solutions.

    Mission
